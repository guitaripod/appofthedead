# App of the Dead 2.0: The Judgment (2026-11-04)

Written from a six-track research fan-out (competitors, AI god-chat apps, ASO, viral mechanics, monetization, Apple platform) with a skeptical recheck, the store and sales data in `myyty` and `asc`, and a teardown of every screen of 1.4.16.

## 1. Where 1.x stands

- About 1 first-time download a day, 0 ratings, $0 lifetime proceeds. Impressions (about 80 a day, 95% search) are the bottleneck; page-view and download conversion are normal (`docs/growth-plan.md`).
- The Day-30 gate of the growth plan (50 downloads in 30 days) was missed: 30 in the 30 days to 2026-11-02.
- The product hides its best idea. The name promises death and the afterlife, but the first run is a list of traditions and a wall of text. The headline feature, the Oracle, needs a 3.6 GB download before its first word.
- The look is beige papyrus set in the Papyrus typeface, with smiley-face and key icons for traditions. Nothing in it would stop a thumb in a search result.

## 2. What the market says

- Learning competition is thin. For "mythology quiz" 8 of the top 10 apps have under 100 ratings; "world religions" and "egyptian mythology" top apps have under 5; no afterlife-beliefs app is in the top 10 for "afterlife".
- Death curiosity has real demand: Death Clock: The Life Lab has 17,940 ratings, and the judgment games "Oh God!" (165,133) and "Judgment Day: Angel of God" (143,823) show how many people want to see souls weighed. "heaven or hell" and "judgment day" are live App Store search hints.
- No multi-pantheon "talk to the gods" app has traction; demand for deity chat is proven only for Jesus (Text With Jesus, 3,956 ratings). The Oracle is a differentiator, not the hook.
- Search hints for "talk to gods" are empty; "mythology", "greek mythology", "norse mythology", "afterlife", "world religions", "heaven or hell" and "death clock" are not.
- iOS 27 adds a Search Results asset and a Product Page Header: one image or video that replaces the screenshots in search, where 95% of our impressions are.
- Apple's on-device model (Foundation Models) answers deity questions in character with no download on every Apple Intelligence iPhone; a local test passed 20 of 20 typical death questions as Odin. Apple's Private Cloud Compute model is free to apps under 2M downloads but needs a managed entitlement the founder must request.

## 3. The 2.0 thesis

Lead with the question the name already asks: where will your soul go? A two-minute Judgment weighs the player's heart by the rules of six traditions and hands back a Soul Card worth posting, then opens the whole app for a week. Everything after it (paths, lessons, the Oracle) is the same depth as 1.x, made faster to start and better looking.

## 4. What ships

1. **The Judgment.** Nine short scenes, each asked by a guardian of the dead from a real tradition (Thoth's record, Rashnu's scales, Charon's coin, the Xolo dog of Mictlan, the 42 Assessors, Odin's question of how you will die, Yama's question of what you cannot let go). The answers weigh seven virtues and name a manner of death; the verdict gives the player's destination in Egyptian, Greek, Norse, Aztec, Zoroastrian and Buddhist belief, each grounded in the path's lessons, plus a soul archetype and the guide who suits it. Abrahamic judgment is left to God: the Judgment never sends anyone to heaven or hell. It is the first run, it can be retaken from Home, and its Soul Card shares as a story-sized image with the App Store link.
2. **Underworld design.** A night palette lit by gold leaf, New York serif for display type, Dynamic Type everywhere, tradition colors lifted to read on black, SF Symbols chosen per tradition.
3. **Path map.** Each tradition opens on a winding path of its eight lessons and the mastery crown, instead of dropping straight into the next lesson.
4. **Story-card lessons.** Lesson text is paged into short cards with the key terms collected at the end, then the quiz.
5. **The Oracle, instantly.** Apple's on-device model answers on every Apple Intelligence iPhone with no download; other iPhones keep the Gemma 4 download. Answers are grounded in the tradition's own lessons, follow the person's language, keep a short memory of the conversation, and step out of character to offer help when a message speaks of self-harm. A reply can be shared as a card.
6. **Premium Week (D26, D27).** The Judgment's verdict opens every path and the unlimited Oracle for seven days: nothing to sign up for, nothing charged, kept in the keychain so a reinstall does not give a second week, never for a buyer. The days left show on Home; the end is announced once with the plans.
7. **Daily Omen.** One question a day from the 1,056-question bank, from any tradition, on Home; answering keeps the streak.
8. **Paywall.** The night look, annual preselected with its saving against monthly, monthly, and Ultimate lifetime; single paths stay on the locked path.
9. **Store.** New subtitle and keywords, new screenshots in every listing locale, a Search Results asset and a Product Page Header, What's New in every locale, and a featuring nomination for 2.0.

## 5. Not in 2.0, and why

- **Lesson text in other languages.** About 200,000 words per language; revisit when non-English storefronts pass a third of downloads. The Judgment, the Oracle and all UI are localized.
- **Private Cloud Compute Oracle.** Needs the managed entitlement (founder applies at developer.apple.com/private-cloud-compute); the engine seam takes it in one line once granted.
- **Widgets.** A widget extension needs a new bundle id and profile; 2.1.
- **Leagues and friend streaks.** No network to fill them at this volume.
- **Prices.** Fixed by the founder; 2.0 sells the same 28 products.
