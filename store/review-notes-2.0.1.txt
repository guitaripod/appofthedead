What's new in 2.0.1: Settings is rebuilt. It shows what the person has open (free, the Premium Week or everything), restores purchases and, for a subscriber, opens Manage Subscription; it reaches the Soul Card, the daily reminder and Game Center; it can remove the downloaded Oracle model to free storage; it rates, shares and contacts support; it lists the open-source licenses; and Delete Midgar ID deletes the anonymous identifier and the purchase records linked to it on Midgar's server after a confirmation (purchases stay with the Apple Account).

What's new in 2.0: the app is rebuilt around the Judgment. On first launch it opens the Judgment: nine short questions from guardians of the dead (about two minutes), then a verdict showing where six traditions (Egyptian, Greek, Norse, Aztec, Zoroastrian, Buddhist) would send the person, with a Soul Card to share. The close button skips it; it can be taken again from Home > Face the Judgment or through the deep link appofthedead://judgment, which the in-app event "Face the Judgment" submitted with this version opens.

Premium Week: finishing the Judgment opens every path and the unlimited Oracle for seven days for someone who has bought nothing, once per device. It is not a store trial: there is no purchase, no sign-up and nothing that renews. When it ends the paywall appears once, and it is dismissible.

Purchases: Premium annual and monthly are sold without a trial period and unlock all 21 paid paths and unlimited Oracle chat. Single paths, three deity packs and Ultimate (lifetime) are one-time purchases. The paywall appears after the first finished lesson and then at most every third finished lesson (three offers in all), when a locked path or book is tapped or a locked path's preview ends, from the Premium Week chip on Home, when a deity's 3 free Oracle consultations are used, once when the Premium Week ends, and from Profile > View Paths to Ascension. Every paywall is dismissible; the Judaism path and 3 Oracle consultations per deity work without a purchase, and there is no account or sign-in.

The Oracle runs on the device: on iPhones with Apple Intelligence it uses Apple's on-device model and answers at once; on other iPhones it asks for a one-time model download over Wi-Fi. Every conversation is labelled as written by AI and not religious guidance. A message about suicide or self-harm is answered by the app itself with a link to findahelpline.com instead of a reply in a deity's voice. Nothing the person asks the Oracle leaves the device.
