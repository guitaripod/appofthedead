# App of the Dead — 1.4.7 What's New

Día de los Muertos event (Aztec Mictlan path opens to everyone Oct 24 – Nov 2, permanent unlock for anyone who starts a lesson during the window), the appofthedead://path/aztec-mictlan deep link, and share links now pointing back to the App Store. The rating-prompt change (first well-scored lesson instead of the third) ships in 1.4.7 too but is intentionally left out of these notes.

## en-US

For Día de los Muertos, the Aztec Mictlan path is open to everyone from October 24 through November 2 — no Premium needed. Start a lesson while it's open and the path is yours to keep. Tap the App Store event and it takes you straight there.

Sharing a path or a passage from a book now includes a link to the app, so whoever you send it to can find their way in.

The Oracle's replies now show emphasis and lists as formatted text instead of raw asterisks.
